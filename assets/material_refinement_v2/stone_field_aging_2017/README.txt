2017 STONE-FIELD AGING / NEW BOUNDED VARIANT

One actual text-only built-in imagegen swatch was created and retained as
generated_original.png. imagegen_prompt.json records the exact prompt and tool.
No copyrighted reference photograph was supplied to imagegen or copied into maps.
prepare.py performs disclosed course/edge preparation and infers PBR support maps.

USE A SEPARATE WALL-FIELD MATERIAL. Do not globally replace Exterior Indiana
limestone. Start only with the north facade continuous stone piers, north facade
end pier and north side-wing end infill. placement.json gives detailed limits.
Columns, capitals, bases, moldings, arches, carved ornament, cornices, tower
terra cotta, floors and all 2026 surfaces should retain their accepted materials.

2048-square, seamless 4 m repeat. Eight approximate 0.5 m courses and nominal
1 m blocks; these dimensions and generated stain locations are not surveyed.
Keep image vertical aligned to wall gravity; positive UV v is world +Z.

Maps: basecolor sRGB; roughness linear; OpenGL +Y normal; 16-bit inferred height;
ORM packs white occlusion, roughness G, metallic zero B. Height stays a sidecar.
The micro-height range actually used is <=24 micrometres peak-to-peak.
Broad dark stains do not become height or deep sculptural wear.
