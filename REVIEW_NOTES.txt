HOW TO READ THE ERAS
2017: dated May/June photographs establish several visible conditions, including existing replacement tower windows, absent chandeliers/canopy and damaged interior fabric. The exact stain, loss and graffiti distributions are interpretations. Unobserved room conditions are not fabricated.
2026: visible restored 2023/24 fabric is carried forward where no later change is established. The Study and Yellow Light have completed-2025 design/photography evidence. Single-view retail fragments do not establish complete current suite layouts. No finished NoMad hotel is included.

MAIN GAPS
Private tower partitions and tenants; complete Mezz/youth-club suites; lift cabins/shafts; basements, plant, tunnels and complete stair systems; exact2017 west-room conditions; current west-side construction; complete site grading/planting.
The southern platform/undercroft is also an exterior gap. A separate local-space2017 cargo-core study is provided without station alignment. The old train-shed roof was demolished in2000. A new concrete deck/parking structure is documented in2025, but its perimeter/elevation/support controls have not been recovered. It is not replaced by an invented finished park.

Revision14 integrated recovery WIP: technical and18-image review complete; fidelity/full scope remain open

The original request remains open. This is an evidence-led, partial architectural reconstruction and material study, not a complete photoreal digital twin or a modern as-built survey.

New source-backed topology: the east elevator lobby now has two splayed banks of three lifts, a narrower variable-width plan, an extended east vestibule, matched arcade transition, and retained lateral restroom openings plus the north-approach west branch. HNS scope pixels and the original 1914 lift plan independently support that topology. Pixel registration, six glyph centers, jamb widths, ceiling and portal profiles remain qualified. A threshold band does not prove stairs or levelness. Restroom interiors, shafts and cabins remain unmodeled.

The lobby floor adopts the existing arcade's local .13 m reconstruction datum. The southern connector smoothly interpolates to the already approximate exit-ramp grade so there is no unsupported abrupt model step. This is geometric continuity, not measured modern grading. Neither the north approach nor external threshold has a resolved survey elevation.

2017 lobby boards are plain, with open upper fields. Dated local photographs establish boarding and cladding/plaster loss classes but do not map all six doors or every loss polygon. Ceiling patches, exposed strips and fragments are original interpretive arrangements. The pale retained-cladding surface avoids generic dark staining; exact material properties remain unmeasured. The 2026 floor setting-out/veins and fine lift controls/ornament remain simplified.

The cross-room material audit found 25 material/era entries which schema validation alone did not catch: unsupported procedural graphs exported without color.
