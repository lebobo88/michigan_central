MICHIGAN CENTRAL STATION — REVISION14 RECOVERY WIP

This is an incomplete architectural reconstruction and material study. All18
rendered images passed a WIP recovery review. This is not a completed photoreal
model, a surveyed digital twin, a full as-built interior or construction data.

EXTRACTING THE FIVE PARTS
Every ZIP uses the same Michigan_Central_Revision14_WIP top folder. Extract them
beside one another into one destination to assemble the project. The packed
BLEND files open independently; their used texture images are embedded. Full
GLBs also embed their materials. The compact GLBs are lower-detail review copies.

01 Review Models: two compactGLBs,18 actual renders, coverage and validation
02 Materials and Builder: exact executed source, original maps and provenance
03 2017 Full Scene: packedBLEND + fullGLB; separate UNALIGNED southern local study
04 2026 Packed Source: packedBLEND only; its fullGLB is in part05
05 2026 Full GLB: full-material interchange for the SAME revision14 source

The2026 source/GLB split is deliberate. Neither part substitutes a different
model revision. See PACKAGE_CONTENTS.json, source hashes and the checkpoint
report. Part04 alone opens in Blender; part05 alone opens in a glTF-capable tool.
Older recovery ZIPs remain separate originals and are not silently overwritten.

WHAT REMAINS OPEN
Fine carving, site/vegetation realism, window repetition, detailed floor/material
appearance, exact2017 loss patterns, complete private/tenant/service interiors,
current south-deck registration and authoritative vertical/site controls. See the
48-row era-by-space matrix and source_gaps.json. Missing rooms are not invented.
The2017 southern study has no validated station alignment or current-deck Z.

MATERIAL/FORMAT CHECKS
Both sources passed geometry, UV, contact and public-interface checks. All six
full/preview/compact GLBs pass uncapped Khronos validation. The25 previously
unsupported procedural material/era entries now pass semantic palette/channel
checks. Compact files preserve subtle normal maps. The50 new full-export map
payloads match their authored source bytes. These tests establish format transfer,
not architectural accuracy or exact physical reflectance.

REBUILD
Use Blender5.2.2 LTS or compatible. Extract all parts into the common root and run
Blender in background with scripts/build_station.py, passing -- --era2017 or2026
and --no-render if desired. Models/exports/renders directories must exist.
Python map tools use NumPy/Pillow/SciPy; optional glTF checks use the official
@gltf-transform packages4.5.1 and gltf-validator. Tool binaries are not bundled.
